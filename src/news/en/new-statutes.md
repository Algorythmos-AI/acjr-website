At an extraordinary general assembly on 17 September 2026, the association founded in Paris in April 2024 as « Paix et la Transition Démocratique » adopted new statutes. It is now the **African Center for Justice Rights (ACJR)**.

## A broader mission

The new statutes place the association's work within international human-rights law, humanitarian law and international criminal law. They set six objectives: documenting serious violations, legal analysis and strategic litigation, transitional justice, protecting human-rights defenders, protecting vulnerable groups, and research and training.

## Clear principles

Independence, impartiality, putting victims first, rigour and the protection of sources now guide everything ACJR does.

The change was registered with the prefecture on 30 September 2026.
