At an extraordinary general assembly on 17 September 2026, the association founded in Paris in April 2024 as “Paix et la Transition Démocratique” adopted new statutes. It is now the **African Center for Justice Rights (ACJR)**, the legal continuation of the original association: no new legal entity is created.

## What the new statutes set out

The new statutes aim to contribute to criminal justice, the fight against impunity, transitional justice and the rule of law on the African continent. They set six objectives: monitoring and documenting serious violations, legal analysis and strategic litigation, transitional justice, protecting human-rights defenders, protecting vulnerable groups, and research and training.

## Clear principles

Five principles guide ACJR's work: independence, the universality and indivisibility of rights, impartiality and documentary rigour, non-discrimination, and a victim-centred approach.

The change was registered with the prefecture on 30 September 2026.
